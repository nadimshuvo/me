Atlas

One hundred small sites.

Ten groups. Each one moves.

Water

TideThe water comes back. WellThe bucket goes down. FerryOne crossing. BeaconThe light turns. BarrelCatch the rain. LockThe gates hold the river. OtterUnder, then up. BuoyIt never sits still. KelpThe forest leans. Mill raceThe wheel takes the stream.

Night

LanternA small light, carried. MothAround the lamp. ObservatoryThe slit turns with the sky. Night trainWindows, then none. EmberWhat the fire leaves. OwlIt watches, then blinks. CandleThe flame leans. AuroraColor without a source. InnA room still lit. BelfryThe hour, once.

Craft

PotterThe wheel keeps the clay. AnvilOne strike, then the ring. PressThe platen comes down. BinderyA signature, folded. DyeThe cloth goes under. LatheRound, by turning. QuiltA line of stitches. BlowerA bubble at the end of the pipe. CooperThe hoop closes the staves. NeedleThread follows the eye.

Table

OrchardOne apple lets go. ApiaryThe flight path home. CreameryThe churn rocks. GristThe stone turns on the grain. SpiceA pinch, falling. OliveOil, in a thin line. CitrusCut, and the wheel shows. Salt panThe pan goes pale. CellarA bottle on its side. HearthThe log takes.

Street

KioskThe shutter lifts. News standThe top sheet turns. Clock towerThe hands agree, twice a day. ArcadeA row of lamps, in order. TramIt arrives, and the doors wait. TailorChalk, then thread. CobblerThe last, and the hammer. Post boxThe flag goes up. AwningShade, unrolled. FountainThree jets, one basin.

Rooms

AtticDust in a shaft of sun. PantryJars in a row. StudioNorth light, and a canvas. LibraryThe ladder finds the shelf. PorchThe seat swings. GreenhouseA drop on the glass. Cloak roomCoats, just in from the rain. Map roomThe rose points. Dark roomThe picture arrives in the tray. NurseryThree shapes, turning.

Sound

ViolinThe bow draws one note. DrumThe skin answers. Hand bellIt swings from the wrist. FluteBreath becomes a line. RadioThe dial looks for a voice. MetronomeIt keeps the promise. GramophoneThe record goes round. ChoirVoices, stacked. TuningThe fork hums after the strike. ChimeTubes, in order.

Motion

PendulumIt spends the swing. GearTeeth meet teeth. PulleyThe rope takes the load. Wind vaneIt names the wind. Yo-yoDown the string, and back. CompassThe needle looks north. HourglassSand, with a deadline. SwingA seat on two ropes. Cart wheelIt rolls until the road levels. SpringPressed, then let go.

Paper

LetterpressType meets paper. StampA mark in the corner. PostcardWritten small, sent far. LedgerA line for the day. TicketTorn at the perforation. Folded mapA coast, opened flat. EnvelopeThe flap comes down. BlotterThe drop spreads, then stops. ScoreNotes, in their places. MarginA note beside the line.

Field

MeadowGrass, and the wind in it. VineyardRows that meet. PastureThe gate swings in. DuneThe crest walks. QuarryA block leaves the face. CopseLeaves let go. RidgeCloud, then the line of the hill. FordStones, set where the water is thin. HollowMist stays low. CairnOne stone, then another.
